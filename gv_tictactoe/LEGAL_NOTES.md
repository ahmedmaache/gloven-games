# Legal Notes - Tic Tac Toe

## Template Disclaimer

This game template is designed to **minimize Google Play policy risk** but does NOT guarantee approval.

## Founder Responsibilities

Before publishing, you MUST:

1. **Review Google Play Policies**
   - [Developer Program Policies](https://play.google.com/about/developer-content-policy/)
   - [Developer Distribution Agreement](https://play.google.com/about/developer-distribution-agreement.html)

2. **Complete Content Rating Questionnaire**
   - This game has: No violence, No gambling, No user interaction
   - Recommended rating: Everyone

3. **Fill Data Safety Form**
   - Does app collect data? **NO**
   - Does app share data? **NO**
   - Is data encrypted? **N/A**

4. **Test on Multiple Devices**
   - Use Pre-Launch Report in Play Console
   - Test on different screen sizes

## What This Template Provides

✅ Offline-only gameplay (no server calls)
✅ No personal data collection
✅ No advertising SDKs
✅ No analytics or tracking
✅ No dangerous permissions
✅ Family-friendly content
✅ No gambling mechanics
✅ No multiplayer / no user-generated content

## What This Template Does NOT Provide

❌ Guarantee of Play Store approval
❌ Legal advice
❌ Privacy policy (add your own if you modify the app)
❌ Perfect AI (it's intentionally beatable)

## Modifications

If you modify this template to add:
- Analytics → Add privacy policy
- Ads → Comply with ad policies, update manifest
- Network features → Fill data safety form accurately
- User accounts → Implement proper data handling
