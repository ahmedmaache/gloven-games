# Swipe Avoider - Policy Notes

## Data Collection
This app **does NOT collect any personal data**:
- No user accounts
- No analytics or tracking
- No network requests
- No device identifiers collected

## Permissions
This app requests **NO special permissions**:
- No camera
- No microphone
- No location
- No contacts
- No storage access beyond app sandbox

## Content Rating
This app is suitable for **general audiences**:
- Abstract geometric shapes only
- No violence or realistic destruction
- No gambling mechanics
- No political or controversial content
- No mature themes

## Google Play Compliance
Before publishing, founders should:
1. Review [Google Play Developer Program Policies](https://play.google.com/about/developer-content-policy/)
2. Complete the Data Safety form honestly (select "No data collected")
3. Complete the Content Rating questionnaire accurately
4. Test the app using the Pre-Launch Report in Play Console

## Sound Effects (Optional)
If you add sound effects:
- Use royalty-free or properly licensed audio
- Sounds should be appropriate for all ages
- Consider adding a mute option in settings

## Disclaimer
This template is designed to minimize policy risk but does **not guarantee** Google Play approval. Founders are responsible for final compliance.
